# Module 04 — Logical Frameworks

## L01 — Logical Framework Anatomy
**Outcome:** Explain the structure and purpose of a logical framework. **Bloom:** Understand. **Time:** 50 min.

A logframe compresses programme logic and measurement into a management matrix. A robust version links results hierarchy, indicators, means/sources of verification and assumptions. It is not a substitute for a Theory of Change; it operationalises selected elements for planning and accountability.

Typical hierarchy: impact/goal → outcome/purpose → outputs → activities. The exact terminology can vary, but the logic must remain internally consistent.

### Guided exercise
Inspect a defective logframe containing activities as outcomes, non-measurable indicators and irrelevant assumptions. Mark every defect and explain the consequence for monitoring.

## L02 — Vertical and Horizontal Logic
**Outcome:** Test vertical causal logic and horizontal measurement logic. **Bloom:** Apply. **Time:** 60 min.

**Vertical logic** asks whether lower-level achievement, together with assumptions, plausibly contributes to the next result. **Horizontal logic** asks whether each result has an appropriate indicator, source/means of verification and necessary assumptions.

### Worked example
Outcome: increased employment among programme graduates. Indicator: percentage employed/self-employed six months after completion. Source: tracer survey linked to programme records. Assumption: local labour demand remains sufficient.

### Guided exercise
Repair four rows of a logframe so that result, indicator, source and assumption are aligned.

## L03 — Construct a Professional Logframe
**Outcome:** Create a coherent logframe derived from a Theory of Change. **Bloom:** Analyse. **Time:** 90 min.

### Case lab
Translate your Youth Employment Theory of Change into a logframe. Include at minimum: one impact, two outcomes, three outputs and key activities; one or more indicators for each result level; data source/means of verification; assumptions; baseline/target placeholders where not yet known.

### Quality tests
No indicator merely restates its result; no source is named without feasible access; assumptions are external to the result statement; every output supports at least one outcome.

### Professional output
Submit the completed logframe using the downloadable template.

Weighted rubric score ≥60%, with no critical methodological criterion below Developing.
